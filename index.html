How it works

From measurement to doorstep

  1. 01

    Consult

    Walk in or book a slot. We talk through the occasion, the fabric, the neckline — and set a realistic date.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Every measurement taken by hand and saved to your file. Your next order needs nothing more than a phone call.

  3. 03

    Cut & Stitch

    Pattern drafted, fabric cut, garment assembled and hand-finished by the same tailor start to finish.

  4. 04

    Fit & Deliver

    A trial fitting, any last adjustments, then pressed, packed and handed over — or delivered to your door.

Meet the founder

The hands behind Stitch Avenue

Stitch Avenue Boutique is run personally by its founder. She meets every customer, takes the measurements herself, drafts the pattern and checks the final fit before anything leaves the studio.

That is the whole point of a small boutique — the person you sit with at the consultation is the person who stitches your outfit.
